Privacy Policy

Application: Sentinel — Last updated: 2025-05-30

Sentinel is an Android application that reads your device's GPS location at a configurable interval and sends it to recipients you choose (via SMS or Telegram). This policy explains exactly what data the app accesses, how it is used, and what it does not do.

1. Data collected

Sentinel accesses the following data on your device:

  • Precise GPS location — read periodically while the tracking service is running, and on demand when you tap "Send Now".

No other personal data is accessed or collected. Sentinel does not read your contacts, messages, call logs, photos, or any other content stored on the device.

2. How location data is used

Location data is used solely to compose and deliver a position message to the recipients you configured in the app. The message contains your coordinates, accuracy radius, and a Google Maps link.

The developer of Sentinel never receives, stores, or processes your location. No server operated by the developer is involved at any point.

3. Data transmission

Depending on which recipient types you configure, location data may be transmitted via the following channels:

  • SMS — sent directly from your device using the Android SMS system. The message is delivered to the phone number you entered and is subject to your mobile carrier's terms of service.
  • Telegram — sent to the Telegram chat, group, or channel you configured, via the Telegram Bot API (core.telegram.org). Telegram's own Privacy Policy applies to messages delivered through their service.

You are responsible for ensuring that the recipients you configure have consented to receiving your location.

4. Data storage

Configuration (recipient list, settings) and a delivery history log are stored locally on your device using the app's private storage. This data is not backed up to any cloud service by the app itself (standard Android backup rules apply depending on your device settings). No data is ever uploaded to a remote server by Sentinel.

5. Background location

To send your location while the screen is off or the app is in the background, Sentinel requests the "Allow all the time" location permission and runs as a foreground service (indicated by a persistent notification). This permission is used exclusively for the delivery purpose described above.

6. Third-party services

Sentinel itself does not integrate any analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDK. The only external network communication is the Telegram Bot API call described in section 3, and only when you have configured a Telegram recipient.

7. Data retention and deletion

All data (configuration and delivery log) exists only on your device. Uninstalling Sentinel removes all locally stored data. The developer holds no copy of any data and cannot delete data on your behalf.

8. Children

Sentinel is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy is updated, the new version will be published at this URL with a revised "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
